AMENDMENT NO. 1 - RAJGAD FORGINGS INDUSTRIES
Amendment reference AMD-S005-01 to CON-S005
============================================================

Amendment No. 1
This Amendment No. 1 to the Supply Agreement CON-S005 between Sahyadri Auto Components Pvt Ltd and Rajgad Forgings Industries is effective from 2026-02-02. Where it conflicts with the Agreement, this Amendment prevails.

Clause 4 (Lead Time) replaced
The Supplier shall deliver within 23 calendar days from the date of the PO.

Clause 6 (Payment Terms) replaced
Payment is due 30 days from the date of receipt of a correct invoice and acceptance of the goods.

Clause 8 (Delay Penalty) replaced
For each full week of delay beyond the delivery date, the Supplier shall pay a penalty of 0.5 percent of the value of the delayed goods, capped at 10.0 percent of the PO value.

All other clauses
All other clauses of the Agreement remain unchanged.
